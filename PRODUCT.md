# Product

## Register

product

## Users
HackerRank Orchestrate judges, automated evaluators, and system participants/auditors. They need an efficient, high-performance, and visually distinctive workspace to analyze claim decisions, track API expenses, and explore multimodal discrepancies.

## Product Purpose
The Claims Audit Dashboard provides an interactive, analytical playground to explore visual evidence evaluation, validation accuracies, token usage metrics, and multi-pass VLM reasoning traces. Success is a highly readable, performant, and delightful interface that enables immediate diagnostic insights.

## Brand Personality
- **Voice/Tone**: Structural, mechanical, authoritative.
- **Personality**: Showa-era Neo-Retro, brutalist, tactile.
- **Emotional Goals**: Nostalgia, visual delight, absolute reliability.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS dashboards, saturated dark-mode cards with gradient glows, overly rounded buttons, glassmorphic filters, and typical AI-generated landing page layouts.

## Design Principles
1. **Physical Materiality**: Emulate warm textured paper with offset, tactile physical outlines and 3D shadows instead of digital ambient gradients.
2. **Monospaced Clarity**: Treat numeric metadata and operational statistics as mechanical print, using monospaced font hierarchies.
3. **Restraint Over Cliché**: Reject common tech aesthetics (like high-blur glassmorphism) in favor of high-contrast solid lines and geometric layouts.
4. **Structured Rhythm**: Guide the user's focus using explicit structural boundaries and Japanese stamp accents.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Contrast: Solid black text on off-white paper (`#f5f2eb`), conforming to WCAG AAA contrast standard (≥7:1).
- Reduced Motion: Animations are limited to state-driven expansions and toggle draws, matching user preferences.
